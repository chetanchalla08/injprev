# Candidate footage (awaiting approval)

Freely licensed clips found on 2026-11-20 that are not yet in the set. Nothing here has been
downloaded. Each row needs the owner's OK before it is fetched; the licence goes into
`manifest.json` when the clip is added.

**Sources used:** Wikimedia Commons (CC BY / CC BY-SA / public domain) and the Internet Archive
(CC0 only). **Left out:**
- Pexels and Storyblocks: their terms forbid machine-learning use.
- Mixkit and Coverr: their licences say nothing about it either way.
- YouTube: its terms forbid downloading, even for Creative Commons videos.
- Archive items with no rights statement.
- `Reves topspin.ogv`, which is table tennis.

**Split rule:** each new *player* goes wholly into tune or held-out after a short look at who is
on screen, and before any scoring. The splits below are proposals and assume new people;
a clip turns out to show an existing player follows that player's split. Stroke counts are
rough guesses from titles and lengths, not from watching.

## Expected yield and caveats

- **Downloads:** about 575 MB in total, most of it C1 (243 MB). Footage stays out of git.
- **Strokes:** perhaps 250 to 350, but a good share of the newsreel and 320×240 footage may be
  too small, too cut up or too low in frame rate (old film at 16 to 24 fps) for the pose model.
  That would be logged, not hidden.
- **Variety this adds:**
  - one-handed backhand players: Tilden, Gibson, Federer, Tsitsipas, the 1930s players
  - women players: Gibson, and probably the junior and recreational clips
  - juniors and recreational players: B4, B5, C4
  - the first smash, slice and volley labels: section A
- **Still missing after this:**
  - modern recreational adults filmed from behind or side-on at 60 fps
  - left-handers (only Rola so far)
  - high-ball one-handed backhands, labelled as such

  The owner's own recordings are the only realistic source for these; see `RECORDING.md`.
